Pipe Material Pricing

Pipes are priced by weight. Section weight per metre = π × (OD − t) × t × ρ / 10⁶ kg/m, and rate per metre = weight × rate per kg. Default rate is Rs 150/kg for all pipes. Un-tick “Price by weight” to enter a direct per-metre market rate instead. Blind and slotted (strainer) pipes of the same material and diameter share the same per-metre rate; when a slotted pipe is used in the design, a separate lump-sum slotting item of Rs 40,000 is added under Section 2. Only combinations actually selected in the Pipe Design table appear as items in Section 2, the BOQ and the Detailed Report.

Depth Segments — Soil Type & Drilling Method

Define the well as one or more depth segments. For each segment, choose the soil type (Soft / Medium / Hard) and the drilling method (Rotary / DTH). The DWSS Rate Analysis Norms (2073) are then applied segment by segment, using the appropriate coefficient tables for each combination. Rotary is used for Soft, Medium and Hard formations; DTH is used for Hard rock only, and produces the final-diameter bore in a single pass, so no reaming is applied for DTH segments.

Note: The drilling method selected here drives the internal rate analysis only. Item descriptions in the Estimate / BOQ remain method-neutral; the method is disclosed only in the Detailed Report rate-analysis notes. The soil type selected here is carried through verbatim into the item description.
